# Anselmi on the projection (reply received 1 Nov 2026)

Context. In October the author sent the hdym paper (*The Lee–Wick ghost of Yang–Mills mechanics is unstable at
every energy*; `github.com/foejoseph-lab/hdym`, `paper/main.tex`) to four people in the field, asking in
particular whether §8 — which bears on the classical limit of the half-retarded/half-advanced projection and
finds that the projected manifold has no basin of attraction — poses the right question. Salvio replied briefly
(interest in seeing it posted; he later provided the arXiv endorsement; arXiv nevertheless declined the paper
without inviting a rewrite, and a rewrite for a journal is pending). Anselmi replied on 1 Nov, with Piva in cc and
an offer to elaborate further. His reply is private correspondence and is kept on the author's side; what follows
is a close paraphrase with one short quotation.

## What he says

1. He has no objection to the claims in the draft.
2. The subspace onto which the fakeon prescription projects **is the physical space, by definition**. No physical
   requirement demands that it be a basin of attraction inside a larger, possibly unphysical, space.
3. The parent space is not unique: many parent theories lead to the same projection, and only the result of the
   projection describes physics.
4. This is the key point of departure from Lee–Wick, and — his words — "one of the main reasons why I did not
   understand their approach at the beginning": getting physics from an unphysical theory was always, for him, a
   purely mathematical operation.

## What it means for the hdym paper (the rewrite)

§8's result stands as a statement about Lee–Wick SU(2) Yang–Mills mechanics: the ghost-free manifold is a repeller
at every energy. It is **not** an objection to the fakeon prescription, and the rewrite should say so in his
terms: a repeller in the parent phase space is a fact about the parent, which the projection does not promise to
preserve and does not need to. The question §8 asks — "does the projected manifold have a basin" — is, on this
reading, a Lee–Wick question. The fakeon question is whether the projected dynamics is well-defined and is the
classical limit of the quantum theory.

## What it means for this repository

- It is an external reason, from the author of the prescription, for the framing fixed on 31 Oct: the route is
  quantum, and classical projection work is ħ → 0 check material, not the route. Basin/repeller language is not
  how the fakeon's classical limit is posed.
- When the ħ → 0 check is run, the question is the one the toy already asks — is the classicized dynamics
  well-defined on the background at hand (convergence of the order-by-order projection; where it stops being
  usable) and does it match the quantum result's limit — not whether anything attracts.
- His reply does not touch the C ≠ 0 question. The literature sweep's verdict (no projection of the spin-2 fakeon
  on any background with nonvanishing Weyl tensor) is unaffected.
