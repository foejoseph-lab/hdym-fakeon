# Chronology 1939–2014: the spin-2 thread and the ghost thread, meeting at Stelle 1977

Written 31 Oct 2026 for the author to read and correct before the ladder is fixed. This is the
map for the sweep: a forward pass from the founding ideas to agravity, with the branch points marked for the
back passes. It stops where `notes/2026-10-30_literature_sweep.md` begins (agravity, the fakeon papers,
Donoghue–Menezes, 2014–2026) and points into it rather than repeating it.

**How to read.** Two threads, braided by date. An item gets a paragraph if it contributes a formula or
conjecture rung 1 or our paper will use, a line otherwise. Marks:

- **[v]** reference verified this session against a journal table of contents, an arXiv listing, or two
  independent reference lists; **[m]** from memory, textbook-stable, not re-checked; **[m?]** from memory and
  worth a check before it is leaned on.
- **⊣** dead on the vine: a line of work that stopped, to be revisited on a back pass for anything unspent.
- **⇄** alternative path: a different route that rejoins the main line, to be walked on a later forward pass.
- **✗** a known crack: a published statement later found wrong, disputed, or left as a hope.

Rule inherited from the brief: anything published before 1977 that Stelle 1977 rests on is presumed fact
unless history marked it wrong. Method preferences for rung 1 are in §5.

## 2. 1977: where the threads meet

**Stelle, Phys. Rev. D 16, 953 (1977), "Renormalization of higher-derivative quantum gravity"** [v]; with
**Stelle, Gen. Rel. Grav. 9, 353 (1978), "Classical gravity with higher derivatives"** [v] as the classical
companion. Action R + αR² + βC² (his α, β; agravity's 1/(6f₀²), −1/(4M²) after signature; the map is in
sweep §4). What it establishes, in the order rung 1 will re-derive it:

1. *The propagator.* In a general gauge the spin-2 and spin-0 projectors give three poles: the massless
   graviton; a massive scalar at m₀² ∝ 1/α (the R² mode, positive residue); a massive spin-2 at m₂² ∝ 1/β
   with a residue of the wrong sign. The partial-fraction form 1/(k²(k² − m₂²)) = (1/m₂²)[1/k² − 1/(k² − m₂²)]
   is the whole story: 1/k⁴ fall-off bought with a negative-norm pole. Rung 1 item (a).
2. *Power counting.* With the 1/k⁴ propagator and vertices of up to four derivatives, the superficial degree
   of divergence of any diagram is 4, independent of loop order; the counterterms are the operators already
   in the action (plus the Gauss–Bonnet total derivative and □R). The argument is Stelle's; the proof that
   gauge invariance survives it is his §III–IV with the BRS machinery of the time.
3. *The classical paper* gives the Newtonian potential −1/r + (4/3)e^{−m₂r}/r − (1/3)e^{−m₀r}/r (the Yukawa
   signs are the residue signs) and the bound on the masses from Eötvös-type experiments.
4. **✗ The unitarity sentence.** Stelle states that the theory is renormalizable but that the massive spin-2
   pole violates either unitarity or causality, and leaves it as a hope that the pole might be moved off the
   real axis by interactions (the Lee–Wick route) or otherwise rendered harmless. Every later paper in both
   threads is a response to this sentence. Rung 1 quotes it with its page.

Not re-derived in rung 1, taken as fact: the gauge-fixing and ghost structure (three kinds of ghosts once the
gauge-fixing term is given a fourth-order weight; Barth & Christensen 1983 below spell it out).

## 4. The cracks, collected

1. **Stelle's unitarity sentence (1977).** A hope, not a result; both threads are answers to it.
2. **The one-loop numbers.** Julve–Tonin 1978 (diagrams) wrong → Fradkin–Tseytlin 1982 (background field)
   corrected but the R² sector wrong → Avramidi–Barvinsky 1985 final, confirmed by de Berredo-Peixoto–Shapiro
   2005 and Salvio–Strumia 2014 → Donoghue–Menezes–Percacci 2024 say the question "which running" was never
   asked. Rung 1's generator-plus-checker has to reproduce the 1985 numbers *and* state which running they are.
3. **Lee–Wick Lorentz invariance (1971).** Nakanishi vs Lee–Wick, open for 46 years, closed by 1703.04584.
   The scalar bubble's lesson (i) is this crack met in the numerics.
4. **Minkowski formulation (2017).** Aglietti–Anselmi: no consistent Minkowski-first definition. The open
   item from the scalar bubble; a computation, not a reading.
5. **Gauge dependence of the ghost pole (1986–88).** Antoniadis–Tomboulis vs Johnston; dead on the vine.
6. **Lee–Wick-proper unitarity (2023).** Kubo–Kugo; whether it reaches the fakeon is open.

## 5. The ladder, as agreed 31 Oct

The sweep starts further back than Stelle, at the first paper on the path that computes something, and walks
paper by paper. At each paper the computation is redone **with the technique current at its date, computer-
aided**, then redone with each later technique in turn up to the present, and the results are compared. The
expectation is agreement up to known corrections; where they disagree, that is a finding, about the theory or
about the paper as written. Flogging a dead horse, by design: the point is to see whether each crack would
still crack, and to learn the technique set on problems that are straightforward before it is needed on
problems that are not. The research paper is the focus of the project, not a deadline; whether one results is
open.

Rules carried from the discussion of 31 Oct:

- **Yang–Mills and purely classical work** stay off the ladder unless a paper on the path requires them and
  the author says so in writing at the time. Deser, Tsao & van Nieuwenhuizen, Phys. Lett. B
  50, 491 (Einstein–Yang–Mills) and Deser & van Nieuwenhuizen, Phys. Rev. D 10, 411 (Einstein–Dirac) are
  cited as lines in rung 3, not computed. Lee & Wick, Phys. Rev. D 2, 1033 (1970), finite QED, is cited as a
  line in rung 2, not computed, unless the author says otherwise.
- **Alternative paths (⇄) that end where we end are walked too, one level back**: their founding computation
  is redone the same way, not their whole lineage. Back to the paper, not to the invention of the wheel.
- **Technique order**: the background-field machinery is learned on rungs 1
  and 3, *before* the diagrammatic attempt at Julve–Tonin 1978 that the Stelle rungs will make. So that the
  diagrammatic attempt is a real attempt and not a feint, its stopping criterion is written down before it
  starts, when the ladder is extended past the pause.
- **The fakeon enters only at the finite parts.** Divergences and beta functions are prescription-independent.
  Read 31 Oct evening from the arXiv PDF: Anselmi & Piva 1803.07777 (JHEP 05 (2018) 027) say that the
  renormalization of the fakeon theory "coincides with the one of the Euclidean version of the theory" (§1, §2,
  §5) — not "with Stelle's"; the step from the Euclidean theory's counterterms to Stelle's is ours. Their beta
  functions are eqs. (3.12)–(3.13) with Δα = −133/10 and Δξ = 5/6 + 5ξ/α + 5ξ²/(3α²); they compare with
  Salvio–Strumia and report agreement "apart from the following discrepancies" in agravity's eqs. (54a) and
  (55) — to be read in full, since agravity is our comparison theory (open item). On this ladder the
  fakeon first appears in rung 2 as the last technique applied to the 1971 bubble.
- Every rung: the paper summarised to the author first, one run, then talk, review against intent before any
  independent review.
- **Correction of 2 Nov.** The author's instruction (in conversation, paraphrased): 1971 first; the 2017 prescriptions and model only
  when the timeline has reached 2017 and on the backward sweep from there; no 2017 pairs while still on the forward pass through 1971; the
  sweep goes forward along the timeline, back to pick up branches and to apply later results to earlier situations, then returns to where
  the back began; rungs are convenient turn points only where they make sense. Claude's reading of it, applied here and to be struck if
  wrong: the "later techniques" column above mixes two things — a *regulator or computational method* (cutoff, dim reg, heat kernel,
  momentum residues, Feynman parameters) is mathematics and may be applied at any point of the forward pass, while a *prescription* that
  defines the theory where the integral is undefined (the Lee–Wick contour, CLOP, the region-wise continuation, the average continuation,
  the fakeon) is physics and enters only when the timeline reaches it. So rung 2's forward pass is Lee–Wick (1969), CLOP (1969),
  Nakanishi's test (1971) and the naive real-axis k⁰ as the era's control; 1703.04584, 1801.00915 and Anselmi–Piva's model come on the
  back sweep from 2017. Rung 1 is unaffected on this reading: its later techniques were all regulators.
