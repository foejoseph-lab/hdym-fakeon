# Techniques ledger

The product of the ladder (README, "The rule of the project"): one entry per technique per rung — what it was
built for, what it sees, what it misses, where on the path it is expected to pay off again, and whether it is
standard, forgotten, or ours. Every rung's results note ends by adding here. Entries cite the log they come from.

## Rung 1 — one-loop matter on a classical background (`matter_loop/`)

| technique (year) | sees | misses | pays off again | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hard momentum cutoff on the graphs (1962), both placements | the three orders of divergence as three powers of Λ; the universal log p² (`rung1_finite_*.log`) | covariance of the Λ⁴, Λ² terms with either measure and **either placement** (`rung1_cutoff_{plain,covariant}_unshifted.log`; the placement shift is the same with either measure): the placement changes only Λ², by (1/576)p²(h²+2h_ijh_ij); the Λ⁴ term is placement-independent and non-covariant; and the **finite p⁴ constant (shifted placement) is not transverse** with either measure — the cutoff breaks the linearized Ward identity in finite parts, not only at Λ⁴ | nowhere as a regulator; as the diagnostic of what a non-covariant regulator does, at rung 3; the surface-term formula for shifting a cut-off integral (predicted which orders move and which do not; not the coefficient) is the reusable move | standard, superseded |
| Schwinger proper time on the graphs (1951/65) | all three orders covariantly, **and the finite p⁴ constant transversely** — **only with DeWitt's measure χ = g^{1/4}φ**; with φ the Λ⁴ term is off by exactly ½(ln√g)⁽²⁾ and the finite p⁴ part fails the Ward identity by a local ξ-dependent polynomial (`rung1_propertime_{plain,covariant}.log`, `rung1_finite_{plain,covariant}.log`); with χ its finite constant is MS-bar's plus γ_E times the log coefficients | the Riem² coefficient (Gauss–Bonnet at O(h²)) | rung 3: the measure/gauge-fixing bookkeeping of 't Hooft–Veltman §4–5 is the same issue with more pieces | standard; the measure point is half-forgotten (dim reg hides it by setting δ(0) = 0 — information lost, not simplified) |
| dimensional regularization on the graphs (1972–74) | the log order, cleanly; the full ξ-polynomial of eq. 3.40 (`rung1_dimreg_*.log`); the finite p⁴ part transverse, with constant −46/15 on Ric²/120 [m?: recalled as the 1974 MS-bar value, unverified] (`rung1_finite_*.log`) — **provided the angular averages and the dot products are taken in n dimensions**: taking them in d = 4 is exact for the pole and gives a wrong, non-transverse finite part (a 1 Nov slip, caught by the {Ric², R²} fit failing and a 16π² mismatch; the explicit Ward test came with the fix) | the power divergences entirely, by construction; the measure (δ(0) = 0: information lost, not simplified) | every later rung; it is the common technique from 1974 on. The reusable moves: the loop momentum's n-dimensional l² kept as its own symbol; the linearized Ward identity, in its bilinear form (first-order variation under h → h + pε + εp), as a standing *tool* (rule 6) for any finite part | standard |
| DeWitt's covariant recursion in normal coordinates (1965) | the Riem² coefficient (Euler term −1/360) on a generic background, with no expansion in h (`rung1_dewitt_samples.log`) | nothing at this order; needs the x⁴ term of the normal-coordinate metric to make □R(0) = 0, else the fit degenerates (`rung1_dewitt_quartic0.log`) | rung 3 (fourth-order operators, where the 1985 index-free method is heavy and the coordinate collapse is light); any a₄-type object | standard in 1965, now mostly replaced by the 1985 method; **the normal-coordinate collapse (σ = x²/2, Δ = 1/√g, recursion → polynomial division) and the fit over random exact algebraic curvature tensors are the two reusable moves** |
